1. Task 1 Formülü — 4 Paragraf + Zaman Yönetimi
P1 — Introduction (2 cümle)
Promptu kendi kelimelerinle paraphrase et. Asla kopyalama. örn: "The line graph compares..." → "The line chart illustrates a comparison of..."
P2 — Overview (2-3 cümle)
Hiç rakam YOK. En büyük 2-3 trend / fark. "Overall,..." ile başla. Bu paragraf olmadan Band 7 imkânsız.
P3 — Detail 1
En dikkat çekici veriyi rakamlarla anlat. Karşılaştırma + veri seç (hepsini yazma!).
P4 — Detail 2
Kalan ana grubu anlat. 3-4 veri noktası yeter. Sonuç cümlesi / yorum YAZMA.
⏱ 20 Dakika Planı
0-3 dk: Grafiği anla — eksenler, birimler, zaman, en yüksek/en düşük, trend yönü, istisna.
3-5 dk: Overview cümlelerini zihinde kur + hangi 6-8 rakamı seçeceğine karar ver.
5-17 dk: Yaz (170-190 kelime hedefle).
17-20 dk: Kontrol: tekrar kelime, article (the/a), tense, kelime sayısı.
Yasaklar: ❌ "I think / In my opinion" ❌ Neden-sonuç uydurma ❌ Gelecek tahmini ❌ Tüm rakamları listeleme ❌ 150 kelime altı.
Zorunlu: ✅ Overview paragrafı ✅ Veri seçme + karşılaştırma ✅ En az 150 kelime ✅ Akademik tense (past / present uygun).

3. Paraphrase Lab — Introduction'da Kopya Çekme, Yeniden Yaz (İnteraktif)
🎯 Kural: prompttaki her kelime grubunu eşanlamlı + yapı değişimiyle dönüştür
shows → illustrates, compares, gives information on, depicts, tracks
the percentage of → the proportion of, the share of, the rate of
between 2000 and 2020 → over the period from 2000 to 2020, over a 20-year span
people → individuals, respondents, the population surveyed
✍️ Mini Quiz — doğru şıkka tıkla (5 soru, anında geri bildirim)
Skor: 0 / 5 — henüz başlanmadı
S1. "The line graph shows coffee consumption..." — en iyi paraphrase?
Doğru: B. "illustrates" akademik fiil + cümle yapısı değişti (shows X → illustrates how much X was...). A'da "tell" geçişsiz kullanılamaz, C konuşma dili.
S2. "The bar chart compares the number of students..." — en zayıf paraphrase?
Doğru: C. Bu birebir kopya — examiner "memorised / copied" sayar. A ve B hem kelime hem yapı değiştiriyor.
S3. Hangisi iyi bir Overview cümlesidir?
Doğru: A. Overview rakam içermez, trend/fark özetler. B detay paragrafına ait, C'de yorum yasak.
S4. Process diagram introduction: "The diagram shows how coffee is made." En Band 9 hali?
Doğru: C. "illustrates the stages involved in manufacturing" — 3 üst düzey kelime + pasif-anlatıma uygun isim tamlaması.
S5. Hangisi Task 1'de yasak bir cümledir?
Doğru: B. "probably caused by" = veride olmayan neden-sonuç uydurma → Task Achievement düşer. Sadece raporla, açıklama.
3b. Examiner Seni Nasıl Puanlıyor? (4 Kriter)
📋 Task Achievement
• Overview var mı, rakamsız mı?
• Veri seçimi (hepsi değil, önemliler)
• 150+ kelime, yorum yok
Band 9: tüm anahtar özellikler + net overview
🔗 Coherence & Cohesion
• 4 paragraf, mantıklı akış
• Bağlaç çeşitliliği (whereas, meanwhile, by contrast)
• Paragraf içi gruplama
Band 9: akış tamamen doğal
📚 Lexical Resource
• Trend fiilleri (surge, plummet...)
• Veri dili (accounted for, a mere...)
• Tekrar yok, collocation doğru
Band 9: nadir küçük hata, üst düzey ifade
⚙️ Grammar Range & Accuracy
• Karşılaştırma cümleleri
• Pasif (process/map)
• Tense tutarlılığı
Band 9: çeşitli yapı, neredeyse hatasız

The line graph below shows coffee consumption per person (kg per year) in Brazil, Vietnam and Colombia between 2000 and 2020.

🎯 Odak: Brazil'in dik yükselişi vs diğerlerinin yatay seyri. Overview: Brazil açık ara lider oldu; Vietnam en düşük büyüme. Detaylarda başlangıç-bitiş rakamları + 2010 kırılma noktası.
The line graph compares annual per-capita coffee consumption in Brazil, Vietnam and Colombia from 2000 to 2020.

Overall, while Brazilian consumption surged dramatically to become by far the highest, intake in Vietnam and Colombia remained comparatively low throughout the period. By 2020, Brazil consumed more than twice as much as the other two countries combined.

In 2000, the figures were relatively close, at 2.0 kg in Brazil, 1.5 kg in Vietnam and 1.0 kg in Colombia. As early as 2005 the gap was already visible, with Brazil at 2.8 kg — nearly double Colombia's 1.2 kg. Over the following decade, Brazil pulled away sharply, climbing to 4.0 kg by 2010, whereas Vietnam and Colombia edged up only marginally to 1.7 kg and 1.4 kg respectively.

Between 2010 and 2020, Brazil's upward trend continued, reaching 5.8 kg, an almost threefold increase overall. Colombia also accelerated after 2015, rising to 2.5 kg and overtaking Vietnam, whose consumption levelled off at just 2.0 kg. In other words, by the end of the period an average Brazilian drank almost three times as much coffee as a Vietnamese person.
✔ 184 words — Overview net, tüm paragraflarda karşılaştırma, trend dili çeşitli.
🔍 Neden Band 9? Overview rakam içermiyor ama 2 mesaj veriyor. Her detay paragrafı karşılaştırma içeriyor (whereas, compared, overtaking). Veri seçimi: 10 rakam yerine 8 kritik rakam.

The line chart illustrates the share of electricity produced from renewables in three European countries over a 23-year period.

Overall, all three nations saw growth, but Germany's increase was by far the steepest, overtaking Spain around 2008 and exceeding 50% by 2023. Poland lagged behind throughout, never approaching the levels of the other two.

During these early years Poland's progress was barely perceptible, adding just four percentage points in twelve years, whereas Germany tripled its share.

After 2012, Germany accelerated markedly, jumping to 38% in 2018 and 52% in 2023. Spain also rose steadily but more modestly to 40%. Poland's growth remained slow, roughly doubling to 18%, still less than half the Spanish figure. Notably, Germany passed the symbolic halfway mark, meaning most of its electricity now comes from clean sources — a milestone neither Spain nor Poland has reached.

Doğrusal (linear) üretim süreci — başlangıç hasat, bitiş paketleme.

🎯 Odak: Pasif yapı + sıralama kelimeleri. 8 aşamayı 2 gruba böl: hazırlık (1-3) + işleme/paketleme (4-8). Overview: toplam aşama sayısı + başlangıç-bitiş.
The diagram illustrates the eight stages involved in producing instant coffee.

Overall, the process is linear, beginning with the harvesting of beans and ending with the packaging of the finished granules. The key transformations are roasting and grinding, followed by brewing, freezing and drying.

Initially, ripe beans are picked by hand and then roasted at high temperature to develop flavour. Once roasted, they are ground into a fine powder. This powder is subsequently mixed with hot water to create a concentrated brew, which is then strained so that only the liquid passes through. The used grounds are discarded at this point, so nothing solid carries forward into the later stages.

Following this, the liquid is frozen rapidly to around minus forty degrees and transferred to a vacuum chamber, where the ice evaporates, leaving dried granules. Finally, these granules are packed into sealed jars for distribution to consumers. Because the water is removed by freezing rather than heat, the flavour is preserved — which is why this method is known as freeze-drying.

🎯 Odak: Döngüsel (cyclical) → "beginning/ending" YOK, "the cycle" dili kullan. Aktif yapı da olur (doğa kendi yapıyor): water evaporates, clouds form.
The diagram depicts how water circulates continuously between the ocean, atmosphere and land.

Overall, the cycle is driven by solar heat and consists of four main phases: evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection, after which water returns to the sea and the loop restarts. No water is created or lost; the same moisture simply moves endlessly between reservoirs.

Heated by the sun, water evaporates from oceans and lakes, rising as vapour into the atmosphere. The vast majority of evaporation occurs over the oceans, which cover most of the planet's surface and therefore supply most atmospheric moisture. As this moist air cools at altitude, it condenses to form clouds, which are transported inland by wind.

Subsequently, the clouds release moisture as rain or snow. While some precipitation falls directly back into the sea, most lands on mountains and ground, feeding rivers and underground stores. These flows eventually discharge into the ocean, completing the circuit. Snowmelt in spring provides a delayed contribution, releasing stored winter precipitation gradually over the warmer months.

The plans compare the current layout of a hospital with its proposed design following redevelopment.

The table compares per-capita carbon emissions in four countries in 2000 and 2020, while the line graph tracks how far global temperatures stood above the 1950 baseline.

Overall, developed emitters cut their per-person output, whereas China and India raised theirs markedly. Over the same two decades, the planet warmed by roughly an extra 0.7 degrees.

In 2000, Americans emitted 20.0 tonnes per head, ahead of Australians on 17.5 tonnes, versus just 3.2 tonnes in China and 1.0 in India. By 2020, US emissions had fallen to 14.5 tonnes and Australia's to 15.0, so Australia became the highest per-capita emitter. Meanwhile China more than doubled to 7.4 tonnes and India nearly doubled to 1.9. China's trajectory is the mirror image of America's: starting from barely one-sixth of the US level, it closed the gap substantially within just twenty years.

The temperature anomaly rose steadily from +0.4°C in 2000 to +0.7°C around 2010 and +1.1°C in 2020, an almost threefold increase in the anomaly itself. Notably, the sharpest warming came in the second decade, coinciding with the period when Chinese and Indian emissions climbed fastest — though the data alone cannot prove causation.

5. Band 9 Checklist + Yaygın Hatalar
✅ Teslimden Önce 8 Kontrol
  • 1. Kelime sayısı ≥ 150 mü? (hedef 170-190)
  • 2. Overview paragrafı var mı, rakam içermiyor mu?
  • 3. Introduction promptu paraphrase mi (kopya değil)?
  • 4. En az 4-5 karşılaştırma cümlesi var mı?
  • 5. Tense doğru mu? (geçmiş dönem→past, tek yıl→present, gelecek plan→will)
  • 6. "I / my opinion / because" yok mu?
  • 7. Tekrar kelime var mı? (increase×6 → varyasyon)
  • 8. Article kontrolü: the graph / a rise / Ø renewable energy
❌ Türk Öğrencilerin 5 Klasik Hatası
1. Tüm rakamları yazmak: 15 veri → hepsini listeleme. Seç + grupla.
2. Overview'e rakam koymak: "Overall, Brazil was 5.8..." → yanlış. Overview genel mesajdır.
3. "According to me": Task 1'de yorum yasak. Sadece raporla.
4. "Than" karşılaştırmasız "more": "Brazil was more higher" → dilbilgisi çöker. "Far higher than..."
5. Process/Map'te Present Continuous: "Beans are being roasted" → yanlış. Simple Passive: "are roasted".
